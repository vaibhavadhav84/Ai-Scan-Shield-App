# Design Review
Entry conditions: Runnable screens.
Inspection: Pass 1: hierarchy/task clarity; Pass 2: actual rendered alignment and interaction.
Implementation: Fix reproducible findings and capture corrected evidence.
Quality checks: Both themes, long content, three risk states and empty/error states.
Failure handling: keep diagnostics free of private content; fix the concrete failure, rerun its relevant
check, and mark device/provider absence explicitly. Do not fabricate screenshots or test passes.
Completion: Review record names findings, corrections and unavailable checks.
